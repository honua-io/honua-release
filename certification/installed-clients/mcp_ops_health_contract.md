# Original Ops health primary execution

This release-owned producer covers the existing `ops_health` tool and Ops health
resource for the installed canonical SDK and Inspector: four original rows.
It preserves the original82 denominator and grants no qualification from source
controls or offline vectors.

Each authenticated live read is bracketed by independent observations of the
owned loopback Postgres fixture and Redis stores. The bounded atomic Redis probe
checks ping and cache write/read/delete, hashes actual job records, and reads the
active index. The oracle derives status/backend buckets independently and rejects
missing records, fabricated zero queues, changing records within a bracket,
wrong database/fixture identity, and absent or contradictory evidence postures.
The temporary probe key has a unique owned prefix and expiry and is deleted
before the census. Job, result and index records are never written by this probe.

The actual health response must expose registered database, Redis and self checks,
truthful worst-status rollup, supported section shapes, and complete live health
and queue evidence. Other sections retain their actual unavailable, partial or
not-configured posture. Fresh sessions, exact private credential binding, typed
invalid arguments, unknown resource and anonymous refusals are required.

After an owned same-container restart the producer invokes both clients' health
surfaces again with fresh sessions and newly bracketed store observations. It
checks live recomputation with newer timestamps and unchanged source/image
identity. Health is computed on demand; this does not establish persisted health
snapshots, cross-replica history, deployment success, or operational mutation.
Absent runtime dependencies or actual Inspector invocation wire remain NONPASS.
