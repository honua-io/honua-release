# Hosted-pilot accounting input custody

This directory contains immutable inputs read by funding, registration and canary
accounting checks. Their source bindings and digests remain part of those
contracts. Preserve those bytes until their consumers have a reviewed replacement;
renaming or rewriting historical inputs invalidates their evidence bindings.

The files are bounded observations, reconciliations and reservation records.
They do not establish an empty account, authorize spending, prove an installed
product or qualify a release. Run identifiers authenticate an original source;
they do not grant permission to rerun it.

Unresolved usage, attribution, legacy obligations and cleanup liabilities remain
reserved. Credits do not settle gross liabilities. An observation outside a
collection cutoff needs separate reconciliation; missing or unknown charges must
not become zero. A new namespace does not erase prior accounting obligations.

New collections must retain the coverage window, original source bindings,
response digests and excluded-payload reconciliation. Keep raw responses and
credential metadata in approved private custody. Public projections must exclude
execution input, environment overrides, credentials and personal caller fields.
Redaction must not turn observed history into an absence claim.

Use the current reviewed accounting policy and authenticated ledger for dispatch,
infrastructure and cleanup reservations. Historical reports neither initialize
accounting nor release reservations. Creating resources or rerunning producers
requires the current authorization and cleanup controls.
